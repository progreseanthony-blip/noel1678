Privacy Policy

Global Golf — Golf course construction management

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Global Golf Construction LLC ("we", "us", "our") operates the Global Golf mobile and web application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, professional email address, and password (stored securely) when you create an account or sign in.
  • Project data you enter: projects, quotes and estimates, machinery, materials, instruments, labor records, daily field reports, payroll and invoicing data, and photos you upload from job sites.
  • Technical information: device type, operating system version, and basic diagnostic data needed to keep the App working.

We do not collect precise location data, contacts, or data from other apps on your device.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the App (project tracking, estimates, reports, payroll, invoicing).
  • To authenticate your account and keep your data secure.
  • To communicate with you about support requests.
  • To improve and fix the App.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Your data is stored with our cloud service providers (hosting and database) and is only processed to operate the App. We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and authenticated access, to protect your information. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by writing to us at the address below; we will process verified requests within a reasonable time, subject to legal retention obligations.

6. Children's privacy

The App is a professional tool intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
